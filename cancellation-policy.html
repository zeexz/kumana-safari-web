Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Our Approach

We understand that travel plans can change unexpectedly. Our priority is always to work with you, not against you. If your circumstances change, please contact us on WhatsApp as early as possible — the sooner we hear from you, the more options we have to help.

Specific cancellation terms — including any deposit requirements and refund conditions — are confirmed in writing at the time of booking. The rates below serve as a general guide; your booking confirmation is the definitive reference.

2. Rescheduling

We will always try our best to accommodate a date change. If you need to move your safari to a different day:

  • Contact us on WhatsApp as early as possible, even if you don't yet have an alternative date in mind.
  • Rescheduling is subject to availability and park conditions on your preferred new date.
  • We will always try to match your original package, itinerary, and price on a rescheduled date.
  • Rescheduling with sufficient notice (see table below) is typically free of charge. Late rescheduling (within 24 hours of the safari) may be treated as a cancellation.

4. Force Majeure & Park Closures

Kumana National Park is a living wilderness. Occasionally, circumstances entirely outside our control may require us to cancel or significantly alter a tour. These include:

  • Official closure of the park by the Department of Wildlife Conservation.
  • Severe weather, flooding, or other natural events making safari travel unsafe.
  • Government-mandated restrictions or public health emergencies.
  • Major mechanical failure where a replacement vehicle cannot be sourced.

In any of these situations, we will not charge a cancellation fee. We will offer you a full reschedule to a date of your choice (subject to availability) or, where rescheduling is not possible, a refund of any deposit or prepayment received. We will contact you as quickly as possible if such a situation arises.

5. How to Cancel or Reschedule

To request a cancellation or date change:

  1. Message us on WhatsApp — this is the fastest way to reach us and ensures your request is timestamped.
  2. Include your name, booking date, and the reason for your request (optional but helpful).
  3. We will confirm receipt of your request and advise on next steps, usually within a few hours.

Email is also accepted for cancellation requests at bookings@gokumana.com, however WhatsApp is preferred for speed.
