Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Kumana Wings & Roars ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website gokumana.com and provides private jeep safari tours in Kumana National Park, Sri Lanka. We are committed to handling your personal information with care and transparency. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights.

1. What We Collect

Information you give us directly

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, and email address when you send an enquiry via our website form or WhatsApp.
  • Booking details: preferred safari date, number of guests, accommodation details (for pick-up), and any special requirements you share with us.
  • Payment information: we do not process or store card numbers. Payments are arranged directly (bank transfer or cash on the day); we keep only a record that payment was received.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: your IP address and browser type may be recorded in standard web-server access logs. These logs are not used for profiling and are routinely deleted.
  • Cookies: see the Cookies section below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share with us to:

  • Confirm, manage, and fulfil your safari booking.
  • Contact you with any changes to your itinerary or the park's operating status.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Improve the quality and safety of our tours.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes.

3. WhatsApp & Third-Party Services

Much of our communication takes place via WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.). When you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy. We use WhatsApp solely to communicate with you about your booking; we do not use the platform for advertising.

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts (served by fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google may log a request when these fonts are fetched; please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

We do not use Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics, or any other behavioural tracking scripts on this website.

4. Cookies

This website uses only essential, functional cookies. We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or cross-site tracking. Specifically:

  • No third-party advertising cookies.
  • No analytics tracking cookies.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google's CDN; a connection is made to Google's servers when you first load a page (see Section 3).

You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting the functionality of this website.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain booking records for a reasonable period to fulfil any legal or accounting obligations (typically up to two years). WhatsApp conversation histories are stored on the devices of our staff and subject to WhatsApp's data-retention settings.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data — including using HTTPS for this website — but no transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please contact us immediately if you believe your information has been compromised.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to our legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where we process data based on consent.
  • Object to us processing your data for a particular purpose.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
