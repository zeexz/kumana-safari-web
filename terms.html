2. Wildlife Disclaimer

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Kumana National Park is a wild, natural environment. While we operate in one of Sri Lanka's most wildlife-rich parks and our guides have extensive local knowledge, the behaviour of animals is entirely unpredictable. We cannot guarantee sightings of any specific species, including leopards, elephants, or migratory birds.

Our guides will make every reasonable effort to locate wildlife and will draw on their experience to maximise your chances. The absence of a particular sighting does not constitute grounds for a refund or discount.

Bird migration and breeding seasons are subject to natural variation. We recommend consulting our team about current conditions before confirming your travel dates.

3. Safety

Your responsibilities in the park

  • You must follow all instructions given by your guide at all times. This is essential for your safety and the safety of the wildlife.
  • You must remain inside the safari vehicle unless your guide has explicitly confirmed it is safe to exit.
  • You must not attempt to feed, touch, or approach any wildlife.
  • You must not make loud noises or use flash photography in a manner that disturbs animals.
  • Littering inside the national park is strictly prohibited.

Health & fitness

Safari vehicles traverse rough terrain. If you have any medical conditions, mobility limitations, or other health considerations that could be affected by off-road travel, please inform us at the time of booking. We reserve the right to decline a booking if we believe participation poses a risk to the guest's safety.

Children

Children are welcome on our safaris. Children under 12 must be accompanied by a responsible adult at all times and must remain seated and supervised during the tour.
